Mandatory scheduled replacements
The items shown above are the scheduled replacement items marked “R” in the Windsor user manual for the selected service interval. They are the replacement items used for this maintenance-cost calculation.

Other service/maintenance work: Items such as caliper greasing, brake cleaning, wheel balancing, wheel alignment and tyre rotation are not included in this calculation. They are not listed as mandatory scheduled replacement items in the user-manual schedule used here. Such work may be recommended based on the vehicle's condition or service-centre practice and can also be carried out at suitable independent/tyre service centres where appropriate.

Price information: The approximate prices shown on this page are based on the user's most recent 30,000 km service on 25/05/2026 at Fortune MG Muvattupuzha. These prices are indicative and may change later due to price revisions, taxes, labour/service changes, or other charges. Prices will be updated on this page only when the next service takes place.

Important: Please cross-check the current prices with your MG service advisor before handing over the vehicle for service. The final service-centre invoice should be treated as the actual cost.
Replacement schedule:
• AC filter — every 15,000 km / 1 year.
• Brake fluid — every 30,000 km / 2 years.
• Coolant — every 30,000 km / 2 years.
• Reducer oil — first replacement at 5,000 km / 6 months, then every 30,000 km / 2 years.
• From the 30,000 km service onward, the service charge is approximately ₹2,301. Earlier services are shown without this paid-service charge.
• Inspection-only items are intentionally not displayed on this page.
